🛡️ What happens when you delete your account?

Under Google Play Developer Policy and data protection guidelines, you have the right to request permanent deletion of your Park Solitaire Channel Partner account and all associated personal CRM records.

🗑️ Data Permanently Deleted

  • Profile details (Full name, firm name, contact person)
  • Login credentials (Email, password hash, tokens)
  • Mobile and alternate telephone numbers
  • Registered client dossiers & prospect leads
  • Site visit appointments, logs, and visit notes
  • Support tickets and complaint conversation history

⚖️ Regulatory Retention Disclosure

In compliance with statutory requirements under the Indian Income Tax Act, GST regulations, and RERA compliance, finalized financial commission vouchers, invoice tax records, and paid brokerage billing ledgers may be retained in an anonymized, audited state for the legally mandated compliance period (up to 7 years) solely for tax and accounting audits.
